----------------------------------------------------------------------
CALIBRATION: how does rho compare to real-world reference points?
----------------------------------------------------------------------
  Geidosch (2014) empirical RMBS estimate (rho=0.06): P(AAA loss>0) = 0.0480%
  Basel IRB regulatory mortgage correlation (rho=0.15): P(AAA loss>0) = 1.7450%

  ABX.HE AAA price -> implied loss -> implied rho (base-correlation style):
    2007-02-23 (06-2): price=99.15 -> implied_loss=0.008 -> implied rho=0.134
    2007-12-31 (06-2): price=87.0 -> implied_loss=0.130 -> UNREACHABLE by rho alone even at 0.95 (see note below)
    2008-12-02 (07-2): price=30.34 -> implied_loss=0.697 -> UNREACHABLE by rho alone even at 0.95 (see note below)

  Note: if a target is unreachable by rho alone, that's not a solver bug --
  a single-factor copula's tail loss probability is structurally capped near
  the marginal default probability itself (p_default). Matching a higher
  ABX-implied loss requires ALSO raising p_default, not just correlation --
  i.e. agencies may have underpriced INDIVIDUAL default risk, not only correlation.
